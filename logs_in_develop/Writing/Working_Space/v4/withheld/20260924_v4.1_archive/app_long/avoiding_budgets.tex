are operated at and the two at which every model is measured. Two models were also evaluated under the
full protocol at $\nfe=5$ and $10$ --- analytic average-velocity matching at both, the diffusion baseline
at ten --- and those rows are here, on the same five seeds $\times$ three geometries $\times$ two episodes
and reduced the same way. They are kept out of the chapter because a table in which one model has four
budgets and another two invites a comparison across rows that share no budget. Instantaneous-velocity
matching at $\nfe=5$ exists only on one seed under the protocol and only in the twenty-episode campaign
on five, so it has no row here either.
