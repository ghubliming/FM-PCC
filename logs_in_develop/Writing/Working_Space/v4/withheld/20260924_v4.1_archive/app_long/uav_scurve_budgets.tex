Before the matched grids of \autoref{sec:res:uav:scurve} were laid out, the scene was evaluated with
each model at the budget it is strongest at --- instantaneous-velocity matching at $\nfe=20$, analytic
average-velocity matching at $10$, the consistency-interpolated model at $5$ --- and, without projection,
at one further budget per model. No two of those budgets coincide, so every row below is a per-model
record and none is read against another. The unprojected rows are kept as flown. The projected rows were
flown on the plain constraint set and were withdrawn at v3.67, when the thesis went to the tightened set
throughout (\autoref{sec:setup:tasks:avoiding}); their tightened twins are not run, because
the projection is flown on the configuration the grid of \autoref{tab:uav-scurve} selects, and the two
tables below keep their structure with those cells marked \emph{not run}.
