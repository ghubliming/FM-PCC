\section{D3IL-avoiding at Every Evaluated Budget}\label{app:avoiding-budgets}
\longdata{two tables, every budget under the protocol of DPCC; the paragraph below stays, the tables
move to the web link}

Analytic average-velocity matching and the diffusion baseline were also evaluated under the protocol
of \ac{DPCC} at $\nfe = 5$ and $10$, on the same five training seeds $\times$ three geometries $\times$
two episodes. Without projection their success with constraint satisfaction stays between $0.000$ and
$0.167$ with $14.2$ to $19.1$ violating steps per episode; under per-step projection analytic
average-velocity matching reads $0.833$ to $0.933$ at $210$ to $402$\,ms per control step, and the
baseline at ten denoising steps $1.000$ in $68.7$ control steps at $321.7$\,ms under temporal
consistency. Neither budget lies on the cost frontier of \autoref{sec:res:avoiding:pareto}.

\section{UAV-s-curve under Every Selection Rule}\label{app:uav-scurve-rules}

\autoref{tab:uav-scurve-projection} prints per-step projection on UAV-s-curve at the rule carried into the
controller comparison of \autoref{tab:uav-controller}; \autoref{tab:app:uav-scurve-rules} prints all three. The
three rules cross the finish line on five flights of ten each. The tie is broken by aborted flights first,
because on this scene a flight that aborts stops counting its violating steps, and then by violating steps:
random selection and cumulative projection cost abort on three flights, temporal consistency on five, and
random selection violates on fewer steps than cumulative projection cost, $71.3$ against $74.7$. Temporal
consistency, the rule the flow-based models are operated with on D3IL-avoiding (\autoref{sec:res:ablations}),
has the fewest violating steps here, $37.2$, because more of its flights end early.
