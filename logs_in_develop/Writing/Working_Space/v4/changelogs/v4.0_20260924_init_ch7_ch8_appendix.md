## 3 · Chapters 7 and 8

**Ch 7** (labels `sec:disc:interpretation`, `:negative`, `:threats`, `:limitations` kept; subsection
labels new). Interpretation: *One Result in Three Steps* (the author's three steps and general
conclusion, v3.96/v3.97); *Where the Saving Comes From* (FM K1 dominates too; the average-velocity
objectives add the fewest steps and, on aligning, the outcome; the 12× against the two-step baseline;
the price of a projection is what the projector is handed); *The Budget and the Guiding Steps*
(the two aims pull apart; where they meet); *Candidate Plans and the Selection Rule* (the `-r/-c/-t`
discussion from `notes.txt`: B=1 → identical, B=4 → projection ×3.2–4.9, single-candidate endpoint
291 vs 422–425 ms, the rule per model, the parallel-solve prediction); *What the Demonstrations
Decide*; *The Plant and Its Controller*. Negative results: CI-MeanFM not a general improvement
(ahead by one episode on avoiding, behind on aligning); FM ≈ diffusion on outcome, cost separates;
endpoint projection nothing to win at small budgets; 0/10 in position; no violation-free flight under
the tilt / on the s-curve; a third of the pillars flights collide; more evaluations not always better.
Threats: seeds and sample sizes; training contexts and context 8; what the models do not share (σ = 0.5
prior, endpoint sampler h = 0); **what the time per action contains** (the sim-to-real timing question of
`notes.txt`, answered from `tab:uav-controller-cost`: 5.2 ms controller + simulator step against 8.9 ms
planner at K1; MPC 125.4 ms; IK not timed; no real-time claim); the two projection stacks; generated
demonstrations; the controller comparison. Limitations: positions, not actuator commands; a constraint
is what is declared; endpoint projection needs a velocity field; plan quality read through violating
steps and drawings; scope of the transfer.

**Ch 8**: Summary (three steps, then the general conclusion in one sentence); RQ1–RQ3 answered with
counts; *Towards Deployment* (new `sec:conc:practice`: constraints as a runtime input, the budget as a
deployment choice, positions any controller follows; the two bounds: declared geometry and tracking,
planner time vs execution time); Future Work (demonstrations for the quadrotor — `\hole` for the
simulator name; the controller; real-time execution; constraints from perception; re-tasking at
deployment; the alignment target through the terminal cost; the FM sampler prior; seeds).

Points 6–8 as written: the average-velocity models lead on human demonstrations; FM and diffusion are
level on outcome and differ in cost (never "FM better" where nothing shows it); CI-MeanFM ahead on
avoiding by one episode, behind on aligning, the analytic objective "leads or holds in every
environment"; endpoint projection helps on D3IL-aligning (the hard task that needs the budget), not on
D3IL-avoiding, and on UAV-corridor it is a **trade-off** (v3.96 supersedes the prompt's "or maybe UAV").
