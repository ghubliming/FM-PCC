## KP 2 — the quadrotor incentive is stated, not implied

The draft said *underactuated, open-loop unstable* in five places and never said what follows from
it. §2.7 now carries the consequence, where the manipulator–quadrotor contrast is already drawn:

> What separates the two platforms for a controller that plans is what a bad plan costs. A
> manipulator can stop. Holding the last setpoint costs it nothing, the arm stays where it is, and
> the clearance it had it keeps, so a plan that is wrong is absorbed between the planner and the
> joints. A quadrotor has no such state, because staying in place is itself a flight. An error in
> the commanded position is not held but flown out, over metres of travel, and an attitude that is
> not recovered ends the flight on the floor. The planner is the same on both; the margin for error
> is not.

Contribution 5 in §1.4 carries the one-sentence form and points back at §2.7. §4.3.3 already had the
mechanism (the setpoint integrator is free-running, so plan error accumulates); §2.7 now gives the
reason a reader should care before meeting it.
