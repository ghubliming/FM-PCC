## KP1 — "Is the `src:` line in the abstract just a note that vanishes at publication?"

**Yes, it is a drafting note — but until now it would not vanish by itself.** `\srcnote` was a grey
macro that prints into the PDF until someone deletes it.

**Changed:** the preamble now has a switch.

```latex
\newif\ifsubmission
\submissionfalse          % set \submissiontrue for the submitted build
```

With `\submissiontrue`, every `\srcnote` prints nothing. Every `\hole` still left stays red **and**
raises a LaTeX warning, so missing content cannot ship unnoticed. No note has to be deleted by hand.

## KP3 — "§1.3 *NFE* — explained anywhere? Appendix (v4) or v2?"

**Before:** only in *Scope and Terminology* (§1.5) and by the acronym expansion at RQ2.

**Decision: defining it is a v2 job.** NFE is the thesis's central quantity, so it must be defined in
the body where it first appears, not only in an appendix. A collected glossary is appendix
material, and that part is v4's.

**Changed:**

  evaluations (NFE) of the sampler —"* (the acronym now expands there).

  throughout the thesis."*
- **v4 reminder created:** `Working_Space/v4/REMINDER_20260914_glossary_appendix.md` — a glossary
  appendix listing NFE and the other terms with the body section and label that defines each.
