## KP 4 — §2.4 says few-step generation is a flow-matching development

New opening paragraph: few-step generation belongs to the transport family, because the model is
trained against a chosen interpolation and a straighter interpolation needs fewer ODE steps —
optimal-transport paths `lipman2023flow` and rectified flow `liu2023flow` — while the budget stays a
solver argument. Diffusion has no such handle: its path is the noise schedule, and short budgets are
recovered afterwards by another sampler `song2021denoising` or by distillation `wang2024onedp`. The
curvature paragraph then motivates the two average-velocity objectives.
