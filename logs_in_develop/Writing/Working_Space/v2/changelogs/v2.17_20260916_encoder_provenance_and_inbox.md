## KP1 — "The ResNet encoding for visual aligning is from the D3IL paper — trace where it originally comes from and add it to the thesis"

**Provenance chain (verified, each link from a paper or from code):**

| link | evidence |
|---|---|
| our encoder = D3IL's | `mix_visual_aligning/models/visual_unet.py:39–50` instantiates D3IL's `MultiImageObsEncoder` + `get_resnet` (`use_group_norm=True`, `share_rgb_model=False`, `imagenet_norm=True`, 96×96, 64 features per view). The vendored `d3il/agents/.../model_getter.py` differs from upstream D3IL only by an optional `pretrained` flag (Gen14 U9, default `False`, not passed); `multi_image_obs_encoder.py`, robomimic `obs_core.py` and `base_nets.py` are identical to upstream (diff). |
| D3IL took it from Diffusion Policy | D3IL PDF p. 6: *"We follow Chi et al. (2023) and use a ResNet-18 architecture as an image encoder for all methods."*; p. 15–16: *"… image encoder for all methods proposed in Diffusion Policy (Chi et al., 2023)."* Code: `multi_image_obs_encoder.py` is diffusion_policy's file (diff-verified, `aux_repo/visual_transformer_refs_(Claude_pulled)/README.md`). |
| what Diffusion Policy specifies | arXiv 2303.04137v5 §3.2 *Visual Encoder*: separate encoders per camera view, trained end-to-end; *"a standard ResNet-18 (without pretraining)"*; *"Replace the global average pooling with a spatial softmax pooling … Mandlekar et al. (2021)"*; *"Replace BatchNorm with GroupNorm Wu and He (2018)"*. |
| the pooling code | D3IL `get_resnet` wraps robomimic `VisualCore`: `ResNet18Conv` (fc cut), `SpatialSoftmax` with 32 keypoints, `flatten`, linear layer to `feature_dimension = 64`. |

The Diffusion Policy PDF was downloaded to the session scratch folder **for checking only**. It is
**not** in `aux_repo/PAPERS`, so its entry and the three new ones are marked `NO LOCAL COPY`. The new
entries (ResNet, robomimic, GroupNorm) are typed from the reference list of the Diffusion Policy PDF,
not from memory.

**Thesis changes:**
- **§4.3.6** *The conditioning vector*. Before: *"encoded per frame by independent ResNet-18 towers
  with group normalisation, taken from D3IL [cite]"*. After: *"the visual encoder is that of D3IL
  [Jia], which adopts the encoder of Diffusion Policy [Chi, Sec. 3.2]. Each of the two RGB views, at
  96×96 pixels, has its own ResNet-18 [He], trained end-to-end with the generative model. Its global
  average pooling is replaced by spatial-softmax pooling [Mandlekar] over 32 keypoints, followed by a
  linear layer to 64 features, and its batch normalisation by group normalisation [Wu & He]."*
- **§2.6** *Vision-Conditioned Policies*. Before: *"in which ResNet encoders process each camera
  view"*. After: *"following Diffusion Policy [Chi]: a ResNet-18 encoder [He] processes each camera
  view"*.
- **Bibliography.** 49 → 52: `he2016deep`, `mandlekar2021matters`, `wu2018group`. `chi2023diffusion`
  gained `eprint = 2303.04137` and a verification comment.
- **Left out.** The ImageNet input normalisation (a preprocessing constant), and initialisation
  beyond *trained end-to-end*.
