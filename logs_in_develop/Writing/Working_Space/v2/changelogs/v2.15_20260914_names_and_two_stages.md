## KP1 — "We never say α-Flow and HardFlow. Only in Related Work and by in-place citation."

**Rule, now canonical:** a method is named by its mechanism and credited by `\parencite`. The branded
names *α-Flow* and *HardFlow* appear **only in Related Work** (§3.2, §3.4), where papers are described
as papers. Recorded as rule 1 of
[`Auxiliary/Naming/TRANSLATION_20260914_dev_jargon_to_scientific.md`](../../../Auxiliary/Naming/TRANSLATION_20260914_dev_jargon_to_scientific.md) §8
and in the style memory.

| where | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| §1.2 scope sentence | *MeanFlow and α-Flow belong to it* | *MeanFlow and its consistency-training variant belong to it* |
| §1.4 contribution 3 — title | **α-Flow.** | **Consistency training between flow matching and MeanFlow.** |
| §1.4 contribution 3 — body | *α-Flow [cite] anneals …* · *MeanFlow and α-Flow report …* | *A curriculum objective [cite] anneals …* · *This objective and MeanFlow both achieve …* |
| §1.4 contribution 4 — body | *HardFlow [cite] instead projects …* | *Endpoint projection [cite] instead projects …* (title kept — the author called it good) |
| §2.4 Few-step models | *α-Flow [cite] decomposes …* · *Its target …* · *MeanFlow and α-Flow report / make a small budget accurate* | *A subsequent analysis [cite] decomposes …* · *The target of this family … ; this thesis refers to it as* consistency training · *MeanFlow and this family of objectives both achieve …* · *MeanFlow and consistency training make a small budget accurate* |
| §2.5 Constraint enforcement | *HardFlow [cite] steers …* · *HardFlow avoids this …* · *Per-step projection and HardFlow therefore differ* · *HardFlow corrects the plan …* | *One such method [cite] steers …* · *The method avoids this …* · *The two approaches therefore differ* · *The optimal-control formulation corrects the plan …* |
| §3.7 Positioning (describes this thesis, so the rule applies) | *flow matching, MeanFlow and α-Flow* · *the endpoint projection of HardFlow* | *flow matching, MeanFlow and consistency training* · *endpoint projection [cite]* |
| §4.3 intro | *Flow matching, MeanFlow and α-Flow replace it* | *… and consistency training replace it* |
| §4.3.4 — title | `\subsection{$\alpha$-Flow}` | `\subsection{Consistency Training}` |
| §4.3.4 — first sentence | *α-Flow applies consistency training …* | *The third objective [cite] applies consistency training …* |
| §4.3.6 two-time models | *MeanFlow and α-Flow need …* | *MeanFlow and consistency training need …* |
| §4.5.3 — title | `\subsection{Endpoint Projection (HardFlow)}` | `\subsection{Endpoint Projection}` |
| §4.5.3 — body | *introduced by [cite]* · *HardFlow adds a control …* · *HardFlow discretises …* | *Endpoint projection [cite] is re-derived …* · *A control … is added …, and the constraints are imposed …* · *The method discretises …* |
| `tab:hardflow-setup` short caption (List of Tables) | *HardFlow: published setup vs. this work* | *Endpoint projection: published setup vs. this work* |

**Kept on purpose:**
- **Labels and file tokens** — `sec:method:alphaflow`, `sec:method:hardflow`, `tab:hardflow-setup`,
  `eq:method:hf:*`, and `\texttt{…hardflow…}` paths inside `\srcnote`. Labels never print; srcnotes
  vanish with `\submissiontrue`. Renaming the labels would break v3's cross-references.
- **§3.2 and §3.4** still name both papers — that is Related Work.
- *Flow matching*, *MeanFlow* and *DPCC* stay named. They are the terms of art for the model family,
  the model and the baseline. The author's objection was to the two branded names.

**Check:** a scan for `α-Flow` / `HardFlow` outside labels, `\texttt`, citations and srcnotes finds
exactly two lines, §3.2 (l. 526) and §3.4 (l. 554).
