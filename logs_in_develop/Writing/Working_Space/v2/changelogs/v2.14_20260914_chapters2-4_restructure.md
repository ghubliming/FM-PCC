## KP1 — "Chapter 2: is HardFlow already in the projection part? It feels thin."

**It was one sentence** — *"optimisation-based methods steer the sampler itself"* plus a short
contrast bullet. Now:

- **Ch 2.5 (concept, no equations)** explains HardFlow the way its paper builds it: sampling as an
  optimal control problem (control added to the learned velocity, effort penalised, constraints on
  the final sample only) → one short problem per sampling step, as in MPC → the unknown final sample
  replaced by its expectation given the current state, closed-form for flow matching → optimise over
  that predicted final sample so the constraints act on a plan, not on its image through the network
  → map the correction back → the final step is an exact projection, so the sample is feasible →
  optional terminal cost, active only in the later steps. Then iterate vs endpoint projection.
- **Ch 4.5.3 (math)** gives the derivation in thesis notation, verified against
  `PAPERS/Recommand_Paper/HF/main.tex` §IV:
  - optimal-control problem `eq:method:hf:oc` [HardFlow, Problem 1];
  - posterior means `eq:method:hf:post` [Lemma 1], specialised to the linear path:
    `M_τ(x) = x + (1−τ)v`, `W_τ(x) = x − τv`;
  - the per-step algorithm `eq:method:hf:ref`–`eq:method:hf:pull` [Problems 2–5, Algorithm 1];
  - safety at the final step [Prop. 1];
  - our instantiation `eq:method:hf:ours`, where `C ≡ 0` gives exactly `Π(x̄₁)`.
- The algebra was checked: with scheduler `(τ, 1−τ)`, HardFlow's update
  `τ x̂* + (1−τ) W(x̄)` equals our port's pull-back `x̄ + τ(x̂* − x̄₁)`.
- **Correction:** v2.3–v2.13 said the implemented method is HardFlow *Problem 5*. What runs is
  **Algorithm 1**, derived through Problems 2–5 with a one-step fixed-point inversion. Fixed.
