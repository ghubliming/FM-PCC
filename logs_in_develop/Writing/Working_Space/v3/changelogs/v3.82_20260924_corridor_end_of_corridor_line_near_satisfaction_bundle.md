Every reading was checked against the DA's Appendix C and the corpus:

| reading | result |
| :-- | :-- |

| near bar 0.95 (= 0.90) | tie: 10/10 within 2 % in time per action |
| bar 0.97 | a staircase in which no model is more than two flights of ten ahead of another; FM holds the 10/10 point cheapest (79 vs 93–94 ms) |

beat naive FM"): on UAV-corridor they do not.
