## 3 · UAV-pillars: why the collisions happen, and what a collision ends

- **After `tab:uav-pillars-geo` (Table 6.10)**, the paragraph now opens: "Two things about these collisions are the
  quadrotor's own: why they happen, and what follows from them."
  - **Cause:** the existing facts, reframed.
    - Five pillars are in no declared set, so the projector keeps the plan away from none of them.
    - The demonstrations pass them at the rod's radius, and the map makes that radius the rotor reach.
    - On 50 of 51 collisions the commanded path itself is inside the reach; one is decided by tracking lag.
    - Where they happen: both-hard, third row, 29 of 42.
  - **Outcome:** a new paragraph.
    - The arm stands on a fixed base under position control: an end effector that meets an obstacle is stopped and
      stays under control.
    - A quadrotor is held up by its rotors alone: a rotor strike in most cases costs it attitude control, and it falls.
    - A collision is therefore the end of a flight, not an event counted within it.
    - The simulation does not model the strike. It ends the flight at the first contact (`uav_avoiding_bridge/plant.py`,
      `terminate_on_contact=True`), and the tables read it through the success column.
  - **Nothing is claimed about collisions on D3IL-avoiding**, as the author asked ("the avoiding aren't collision").
    Checked while writing: D3IL's own env also ends an episode when the rod touches an obstacle
    (`avoiding.py::check_failure` → `_check_early_termination`). So the text contrasts the two *plants*, not the two
    *scorers*.
- **`sec:res:uav:pillars:conclusion`** gets the sentence the author asked for. The demonstrations, planner and
  projection are D3IL-avoiding's, and only the plant is replaced, yet the result differs: 0.600–0.700 success within the
  constraints against 0.967–1.000. The difference is the collisions, and both reasons for it (cause and outcome, as
  above) are the quadrotor's own.
- **§6.3.5 pillars paragraph and "The benchmark":** one clause each (the plans pass those pillars within reach; a
  collision, catastrophic for a quadrotor, ends the flight).
