   figure therefore shows that geometry alone and says so, rather than showing three panels of which two
   carry no information. Recorded as `AVOIDING_RAW_GEOMETRY`.

## 1. §5.5.1 — the protocol, exactly

🔴 **The draft called ten episodes "the published number of episodes". It is not.** The paper reports
results "averaged over five training seeds and **ten test seeds** for each constraint set formulation"
(\parencite[Sec.~6.1]{romer2025diffusion}) — **fifty rollouts per geometry**. Our two settings are:

| in this thesis | what it is | against the paper |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| ten episodes per geometry | 5 training seeds × **2** episodes | **smaller** than the published fifty |
| one hundred episodes per geometry | 5 training seeds × **20** episodes | **twice** the published fifty |

§5.5.1 now sets both out as a description list, says which results use which, and states that neither
reproduces the published fifty exactly — which is what `tab:target` has to be read against. The
`\provisional` that claimed otherwise is rewritten, and a sentence was added to say why the headline
results are at one hundred: a rate of $1.00$ at ten episodes is not evidence of a rate of $1.00$.

The same wrong label was in the figure pipeline — `AVOIDING_T1_PROTOCOL` ended in "(the published
protocol)". Removed, with the arithmetic recorded next to it so it cannot come back.

## 4. Figure 6.4 rebuilt

`fig_avoiding_projector_cost` carried five problems, all now fixed:

- **banned vocabulary on the canvas** — "the endpoint **arm**", "one **genuine** step", "hollow =
  **degenerate**", "not citable". Replaced with the mechanism: *guiding step*, and a legend that says
  what hollow means rather than labelling it with a tier word;

- 🔴 **a semantic error**: at $\nfe=2$ **both** series were drawn hollow. Degeneracy is a property of
  endpoint projection only — per-step projection acts on every step and is well posed at every budget —
  so the figure implied a limitation per-step projection does not have. Only the endpoint series is
  hollow now, and the builder says why;
- **a truncated subtitle**, clipped past about 100 characters at this canvas width;
- the axis now names its unit (`network evaluations per plan`).
