## Item 0 — SVG or PNG?

The draft includes every figure by stem (`\includegraphics{fig_x}`), so the compiler picks the extension. pdflatex
reads `.pdf/.png/.jpg` and cannot read SVG. `bundle/make_bundle.py` therefore ships the `.png` beside the `.svg` and
its graphics shim tries `.pdf`, then `.png`; the `--svg-package` bundle instead renders the SVGs through
`\usepackage{svg}`, which needs Inkscape and shell-escape (Overleaf has both; nothing here does). The vector route
without shell-escape is `plotting/svg/svg2pdf.sh` (rsvg-convert / inkscape / cairosvg — none installed in this
container) → `.pdf` in the store → export → bundle. So: SVG is the source of every generated figure and is preferred;
what compiles today is the PNG (3× raster) because no converter exists here. Nothing changed for this item.
