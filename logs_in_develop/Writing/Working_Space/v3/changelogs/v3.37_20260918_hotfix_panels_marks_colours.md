## 1. `fig:constraints-uav`: the corridor and the s-curve drawn like the pillars

The pillar panel was legible and the other two were not, and the reason was the shading rather than the
geometry. Each halfspace was drawn twice — the whole far side filled dark (opacity $0.34$) *and* the
inflation band filled light on top — so a corridor panel was mostly shading and the free channel, the one
thing the panel exists to show, was the hardest region to find.

The panels are now built the way the pillar panel always was: **the obstacle drawn once**, **one grey
band** for the vehicle radius around it, the tightened boundary dashed, and everything the plan may use
left white. The obstacle line stays slate rather than red, because these same panels are the backdrop of
the flown-path figures, where red means a flight that entered an obstacle — a red wall line would read as
a red path. (The small red pillar cores never had that problem and keep their colour.) The far side of a halfspace keeps a faint wash at opacity $0.10$, because a halfspace does exclude
everything beyond it and the slide is not a wall whose end one can see. Legend rebuilt to match (four items: the obstacle,
the inflated band, the wash, the tightening).

`scenes._uav_constraint_panel` is shared, so `fig:expert-uav` and the three flown-path figures
(`fig:uav-corridor-paths`, `fig:uav-pillars-paths`, `fig:uav-scurve-paths`) get the same treatment — which
is the point, since a path is now drawn over a panel the reader can see through. Caption of §5.2.3
rewritten to the new drawing, ending on what the white area means.

## 2. Two different marks, for two different things

New macro `\baselinemark` (a filled circle, `\bullet`) beside the existing `\selectedmark` (the
triangle). They are deliberately different shapes because a table can carry both and they mean opposite
things:

* **triangle** — the operating point *this thesis* carries forward;
* **circle** — the **baseline of record**: the diffusion model of DPCC at its own budget of twenty and its
  own best selection rule, the row every claim in the chapter is measured against.

Applied to the diffusion $\nfe=20$, cumulative-projection-cost row of `tab:avoiding-dpcc-protocol` and of
`tab:state-headline`. In `tab:state-headline` that row had been bold, which collided with the bold used
for "best rule of a model"; the bold is now gone from it and belongs to instantaneous-velocity matching
alone. Both captions say what each mark means.

## 3. `fig:avoiding-tradeoff`: hues instead of shades

`ENGINE_COLOUR` is a slate family, which is right for the constraint figures — there colour separates
obstacle from vehicle from path, and model identity never enters. This figure puts **three models in one
scatter**, and `#5d6d7e`, `#34495e` and `#17202a` are three greys of one hue: in print the legend was
unreadable and so was every panel.

New `sources.ENGINE_COLOUR_DISTINCT`, used by this figure and by `fig:avoiding-raw-models` **and nowhere
else**: MeanFM blue `#1f6fb2`, FM orange `#d95f02`, CI-MeanFM teal `#0f8b8d`, and the baseline staying
near-black `#17202a` as it is everywhere else in the thesis. Marker shape still carries the selection
rule, so the figure survives grayscale as before.

## 4. `fig:avoiding-raw-models`: type that can be read at `\linewidth`

The chart was built at font scale $1.0$ and printed at `\linewidth`, so its category labels left the
press at roughly five points. Rebuilt at $760\times520 \to 900\times560$ with `font=1.45`, and the
in-figure sizes raised with it: category labels $7.8 \to 10.5$, the budget line $8.6 \to 10.5$, the step
count $9.5 \to 10.5$, the value $11.5 \to 12.5$, with the row spacing opened from $11$ to $19$ px so the
three-line labels do not touch. Top margin raised to $78$ px and the subtitle shortened, because the
first bar's step label used to collide with it. Bars take the palette of item 3, so the two
consistency-interpolated bars are now teal shades and MeanFM, FM and the baseline are separate hues.
