## 1 · "Pure QA: does the corridor really slide in z as well? Show the z trajectory in the thesis; ensure the output also moves in z because of the projection" → into the thesis

**Answer, from the geometry and the corpus of record.** No. The corridor of the thesis (`corridor_v2_slide`,
`config/uav_projection.yaml`) has three halfspaces (two wall faces at $y=\pm0.95$ and the slide from
$(-2, 0.95)$ to $(2, -0.05)$, i.e. $14^\circ$ in the $x$–$y$ plane) and four wall-end caps, all of them
`dimensions: [x, y]`. Altitude enters only through the workspace box $z\in[0.30, 1.80]$, which after the
$0.31$ m inflation is $[0.61, 1.49]$. The flown altitude on the 96 flights of the four paired cells lies in
$[0.86, 1.37]$ m, so the box never binds. Chapter 5 already said "altitude does not enter, because every
constraint here is horizontal" (caption of `fig:expert-uav`); it is now said where the corridor is defined and
where its results are read.

**The projected output does move in z — as a by-product, not a constraint response.** Recounted from the staged
rollouts (`temp/18-09-2026/logs/UAV_MIX/uav-corridor`, `obs_all[:, 5]`, `diffuser` vs
`dpcc-t-bounds_free-pdes-tightened`, same launches):

| model, $\nfe$ | launch $z$ | end $z$ unprojected | end $z$ projected | max paired $|\Delta z|$ per flight | mean $\Delta z$ at $x\in[1,2)$ |
| :-- | --: | --: | --: | --: | --: |
| MeanFM 3 | 1.110 | 1.029 | 1.111 | 0.224 | +0.048 |
| CI-MeanFM 3 | 1.110 | 1.028 | 1.100 | 0.212 | +0.051 |
| FM 3 | 1.110 | 1.091 | 1.066 | 0.137 | −0.022 |
| Diffusion 20 | 1.110 | 1.163 | 1.246 | 0.275 | +0.084 |

The difference grows along $x$ towards the exit, where the slide is tightest, and stays $0.3$ m or more inside
the box. Why it exists at all: the projected sample is regenerated after each lateral correction, and this
scene's configuration releases the action box (`-bounds_free`), so $z$ is free to follow. (In Gen15 U11–U13,
with the action cap on, the same projector moved $z$ by at most $0.001$ m — the cap derived from a
zero-variance $dz$ in the demonstrations was the reason; see `Gen15/U12/CLOSURE_20260912_…`.)
