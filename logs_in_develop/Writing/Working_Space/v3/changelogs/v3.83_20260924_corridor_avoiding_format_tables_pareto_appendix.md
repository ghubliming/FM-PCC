## 3 · The Pareto result (author's v3.82 item 1.1, answered again)

The frontier convention of D3IL-avoiding compares control steps and time at equal satisfaction. On it, at the corridor's
near-satisfaction bar, **the average-velocity models dominate FM at the cheapest budget**:

| constraint | configuration | steps | ms/step | FM at the same budget |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| tilt | MeanFM K1 | 253.2 ± 4.4 | 27.4 | 259.8 ± 9.2 at 27.6 ms (margin below FM's spread) |
| hump | CI-MeanFM K3 endpoint | 284.6 ± 2.8 | 71.9 | 299.3 ± 4.7 at 71.9 ms (margin 3× the larger spread) |

- The frontier:
  - tilt: MeanFM K1 → CI-MeanFM K1 → CI-MeanFM K2 → FM K3 per-step (fewest steps, 247.2 at 79.2 ms);
  - hump: CI-MeanFM K3 endpoint → FM K3 per-step (277.0 at 77.1 ms).
- The baseline is dominated, with more steps at 9–24× the time.
- **Bounds:**
  - at 97 % the tilt frontier is FM K3 per-step alone, while the hump part holds;
  - at the strict count only the baseline is in the band (tilt 0.8, hump 1.0);
  - in time per action alone, the three flow models tie (DA Appendix C).
- **Before projection:** FM K1 (cheapest) and CI-MeanFM K1 (fewest steps) are non-dominated, and CI-MeanFM K1
  dominates MeanFM K1.
