## 9 · The style hint gains the second alert and the tone

- 🔴 **RED — the prompt is in the thesis.** The three shapes, unchanged from v3.51.
- 🟡 **YELLOW — self-reasoning** (new). A measurement does not come with its own explanation: when a
  sentence gives a number and then a *because*, ask which of the two the evaluation produced. Worked
  example is item 8 above, plus the family it belongs to — defending an unchallenged choice, explaining
  away a weak result, telling the reader what to conclude, narrating the writing. And the exception: a

  sentence beside it).
- **The tone the author wants** (new), from his own words: *"you just fact truth telling, and story
  telling, you are the boss ... not the self explosion suicide way."* Write as the person who ran the
  experiments. State it; tell it as a story, not a defence; do not argue with a reader who is not
  there; do not apologise for the sample. A real limit goes once into a `\guard`, flatly — *a limit
  stated plainly is strength; a limit argued around is not.* With the guard that this is not licence to
  overclaim.
