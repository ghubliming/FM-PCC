## Key points

1. **§6.1 names the selection as an MPC choice** and cites v2: at each control step the planner generates
   $B=4$ candidate plans and executes one, under the three rules of `sec:method:selection`. v2 already
   defines them (random, temporal consistency, cumulative projection cost, with the equations), so no v2
   note was needed.
2. **§6.1's second paragraph was a conclusion in the introduction.** Replaced by two sentences of roadmap
   with no results in them; the operating-point result now appears where it is established.
3. **§6.1.1 distinguishes the two step budgets.** For the diffusion baseline $\nfe$ counts denoising steps of
   the reverse process, fixed when the noise schedule was discretised at training; for the flow-based models
   it counts ODE solver steps chosen at inference. Sent to v4 for the glossary.
4. **The "missing" DPCC-protocol rows were not missing.** `Folder_Name` does not carry the backbone, and the
   2-episode MeanFlow cells exist under both `bbmf_dit` and `bbunet` model folders with the *same*
   `Folder_Name`; selecting on the folder name alone mixed them and hid the architecture-matched rows.
   `avoiding_rules_by_protocol.py` now selects on (folder, backbone substring of `Full_Path`).
   **Table 6.1 gains six rows**: MeanFM at $\nfe=1$ — $0.900$ / $0.933$ / $0.967$ S&C for $r$/$c$/$t$,
   $58.6$ control steps and $18.3$\,ms at its best rule — and at $\nfe=2$ — $0.967$ / $0.933$ / $0.967$,
   $59.4$ steps. Five seeds × two episodes, all three geometries, temporal U-Net. Still pending: FM at
   $\nfe=1,2$ and CI-MeanFM at $\nfe=1,2$ (the 5-seed α-Flow cells there are `bbsit` with `ae0.0`, i.e.
   MeanFlow's target, and the U-Net model exists for seed 6 only). Ledger R8 🔴 → 🟡.
5. **The guard under Table 6.3 was rewritten as a result.** It now states what the table shows — the two
   average-velocity models reach full success at two and five evaluations against the baseline's twenty,
   $19.6\times$ and $2.5\times$ cheaper per control step, three of four within a step of each other on path
   length — and the remaining guard carries only the sample size and the by-construction budget difference.
6. **§6.1's caveat now names the inverse kinematics** as the element that absorbs plan error, and reads
   DPCC's Table 2 as the measurement of how much: a dynamics model wrong by a factor of four still satisfies
   the constraints in three quarters of episodes, so the downstream margin is far larger than any difference
   between two generative models trained on the same data.
7. **§6.2.1 leads with the finding**, not with numbers: one model moves the box to its target and three
   barely move it; the paired margins follow in the second paragraph.
8. **Table 6.6 marks its selected row** (MeanFM at $\nfe=20$) with the same bold triangle as the avoiding
   tables, and its caption defines the mark.
9. **Model order is MeanFM, CI-MeanFM, FM, Diffusion** in every Chapter 6 table that lists models
   (6.2, 6.3, 6.6, 6.8, 6.9, 6.10, 6.11), matching Table 6.8.
10. **UAV success is now "the goal passed"** — the vehicle crosses the finish line of its scene, the
    criterion of D3IL-avoiding transferred to the air, reported with collision-free flight as S&C. §5.5.3
    defines it and says so; the strict *within 0.30 m of the goal point* criterion is dropped from the thesis
    and from `uav_results.py`, which now prints the crossing criterion everywhere. The final distance to the
    goal point is kept as a descriptive quantity, and is what the controller caveat rests on.

## What the criterion change did to the numbers

- **UAV-corridor:** unchanged in substance. Every model passes the goal; the separation is collision-free
  passing (MeanFM and CI-MeanFM $12/12$ from $\nfe=3$; FM at most $2/12$; the baseline collision-free on all
  twelve but passing on none). The table drops the strict column and gains a *Passed* column.
- **UAV-pillars: the ordering of the two leading models swaps.** At the strict criterion MeanFM led
  (mean $0.618$ against FM $0.327$). At the goal-passed criterion **FM leads** (mean $0.718$, ceiling $1.00$,
  8 of 11 configurations $\ge0.8$) against MeanFM ($0.627$, ceiling $0.90$, 5 of 11), with CI-MeanFM $0.364$
  and the baseline $0.08$. MeanFM keeps the shorter path: $442$ against $562$ control steps at each model's
  best projected configuration. The section, both pillars tables, the recap tables and the Chapter 8 draft
  now report this as a trade rather than a lead, and the selected pillars combination becomes FM with
  endpoint projection on a single candidate ($1.00$ at $78$\,ms per control step).
- **UAV-s-curve:** the listing is now *passed* rather than *goal reached*; no flight is collision-free, so
  S&C stays $0.00$ throughout. In the controller caveat, MuJoCo MPC passes the goal on two of three flights
  and ends $0.29$\,m from the goal point against $2.81$\,m for brake-to-rest.
