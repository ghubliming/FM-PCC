**The bug.** The extract marked a flight as having reached the goal from `success_strict` — the
0.30 m goal-point criterion the author replaced in v3.28 with crossing the finish line. Every panel
subtitle therefore disagreed with the tables it sits next to: the corridor figure read *8/12 reached*
where `tab:uav-corridor` reads *12/12 passed*, and the pillars panels read 9/10 where the text says
every model crosses on nearly every flight. `extract/uav_paths.py` now reads `success_relaxed`, the
field `analysis/uav_results.py` reports, and the legend and subtitles say "passed" rather than
"reached". After the re-extract **every panel count matches its table cell** — corridor MeanFM $\nfe=1$
12/12 passed and 2/12 clean, FM $\nfe=5$ 12/12 and 2/12, CI-MeanFM $\nfe=1$ 12/12 and 4/12, baseline
0/12 and 12/12; pillars 10/10 passed everywhere with 9, 10, 9 and 3 clean; s-curve 2/3 and 0/10.
Recorded in `plotting/NOTEBOOK_20260915_figure_data_sources.md`.

**Captions written against the drawings, not against the request.** Three traps named in the DA note
are handled: the s-curve caption says both panels are the *unprojected* plan, so the comparison is of
the controller alone, and that neither controller is collision-free there; the pillars caption states
explicitly that the four panels are **not** under the same projection and that each subtitle names its
own; the corridor caption names the one configuration all ten panels share (per-step projection with
temporal consistency on tightened constraints, the configuration `tab:uav-corridor` reports).

**New prose, from the drawings.** Two sentences that the tables cannot carry: on UAV-pillars the models
do not fail by stopping short but by committing to a side — instantaneous-velocity matching sends all
ten flights above the pillar field and keeps all ten clean, the two average-velocity models send nine
below and one above and it is the one above that clips a pillar, and the diffusion baseline flies the
upper lane but touches the pillars on seven flights of ten (verified per flight from
`data/uav_paths.json`). On UAV-corridor the figure shows the trade as one picture: the budget at which
a model stops touching the slide is the budget at which the baseline still stops short.
