### Fig. 6.2 at two episodes, without a new fetch

The staged artefacts are twenty-episode runs, and there are no two-episode ones on disk. There did not
need to be: `scripts/eval.py:297--299` sets `torch.manual_seed(i)` and `env_seed = i`, so **episode $i$
of a run is fixed by $i$** and the first two episodes of a twenty-episode artefact *are* the two a
two-episode run produces. The extractor takes the first `AVOIDING_EPISODES = 2`, and the `\dataref`
records the seeding so a reader can check it. It also reads better: four panels of two paths instead of
four of twenty. $2/2$ reach the goal in every panel, all violation-free.
