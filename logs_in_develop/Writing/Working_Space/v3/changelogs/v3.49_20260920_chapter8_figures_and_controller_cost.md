## 1 · §6.3.3 — a time comparison, so the tracking-controller caveat says *why* MuJoCo MPC is not adopted

The subsection already showed that MuJoCo MPC flies UAV-s-curve where the cascaded geometric
brake-to-rest controller inverts the vehicle on every flight. What it did not say is what that costs,
which is the reason the thesis keeps the cheaper controller everywhere.

New **`tab:uav-controller-cost`**, on the unprojected arm where nothing lies between plan and controller:

| Time per executed control step [ms] | Cascaded geometric, brake-to-rest | MuJoCo MPC |
| :-- | --: | --: |
| The control loop | 94.9 | 215.6 |
| — the planner | 88.4 | 89.8 |
| — the controller and the simulator step | 6.5 | 125.8 |

**About 119 ms per control step, roughly twenty times, is the controller.** The planner's share is the
same in both columns, so the difference is the tracker: MuJoCo MPC solves an optimisation problem every
control step where the geometric law evaluates a formula. On its own it costs more per control step than
generating the plan — about seven times what the D3IL-avoiding operating point spends on an entire
action (18.1 ms). And it buys a traverse, not a result: no flight of this scene passes the goal within
the constraints under either controller.

- **Source.** `Report_20260909_MJPC_vs_PID_s_curve` §5.4–5.5 (Campaign `DA_20260909_T5_mjpc_vs_pid_s_curve`).
  Elapsed flight time from the job records ÷ executed control steps (the divergence step where the guard
  stopped the flight, otherwise the step the goal was passed at).
- **Written as a different quantity from the rest of the chapter, in the caption and in the prose.** Ch 5
  defines the reported time *around the planner* (`sec:setup:metrics:avoiding`); it prices no controller.
  This table is the only place in Chapter 6 where a number contains one.
- **`\guard`:** a decomposition of recorded elapsed times, not a timer around the controller call, which
  the evaluation does not instrument; the remainder row carries the simulator step, so only the
  *difference* isolates the controller. One training seed, ten flights against three.
- The report's Fisher-exact *p* is **not** carried into the draft (no statistical tests in this thesis).
