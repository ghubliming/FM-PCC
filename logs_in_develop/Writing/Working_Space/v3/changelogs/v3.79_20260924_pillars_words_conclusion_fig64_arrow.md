## 1 · What "contact" was, and one word per outcome

"Contact" was a **failure, not a violation**: a flight in which the quadrotor hits a pillar ends there and does not reach
the goal line; the pillars it hits are in no constraint set, so no constraint is violated. The column equalled one minus
the success rate. The confusion had a second source: the quadrotor metric called "no constraint violated"
**collision-free**, so a flight could be "collision-free" and end in a pillar contact at once (Fig 6.5's legend said
exactly that).

## 2 · §6.3.1.3 Conclusion (`sec:res:uav:pillars:conclusion`, new)

- **Does changing only the plant work, and why?** Yes for the declared constraints: before projection the flown plans
  violate them as on D3IL-avoiding, after projection neither violates them, at the same steps (within three) and time
  (within a millisecond). Why: the planner, plan, observation, projection and constraint rows are D3IL-avoiding's, in its
  coordinates; only the executing plant differs (`sec:method:env:uav`, v2 §4.6.3); the arena is the table under the
  similarity map, written inline ($X = s(y_a − y_0)$, $Y = −s(x_a − x_0)$, $s = 36$ — v2's `eq:method:env:pillarsmap`,
  not referenced by label because the review bundle collapses Ch 4 and would print "??"); the vehicle's flown position
  stays within ~0.007 of the commanded one in table units (manipulator 0.02–0.04), inside the 0.025 tightening margin.
  What the swap changes: the scale sends the rod's radius onto the rotor reach, the five pillars outside the constraint
  sets are projected against on neither platform, so skimming a post becomes a collision.
- **Does D3IL-avoiding's model + projection relationship hold?** Where testable, yes: CI-MeanFM stays one flight in thirty
  ahead of MeanFM on both (1.000/0.967; 0.633/0.600, 0.700/0.667), within the seed spread; per-step projection still
  removes the violations; collisions hit both models alike. Not tested: the baseline and FM were not flown, so the
  dominance over the baseline of record stays a D3IL-avoiding result.
