lines, three lanes, one altitude per episode; `tab:uav-demos`).
- Unprojected, the flights cannot be told apart.
- Projected, the three objectives are within 1.6 violating steps per flight at a shared budget and projection method.
- The budget takes them from ~10 violating steps (K1) to 1–2 (K20) under the tilt.
- Against the baseline: every flight reaches the end, at 1/24 (tilt) and 1/9 (hump) of its time per action. None leaves
  as few violating steps: the flow models' best is 1.4 and 0.2, the baseline's 0.8 and 0.0.
