## 2 — "Table 6.1 is crashed in the PDF, out of the page margin"

**Cause, found:** a plain `tabular` is as wide as its widest row, and the table's two note rows were
`\multicolumn{7}{l}{...}`, which never wraps. The longer note was about **20 cm** against a text width of
about 14.7 cm.

**Fix:** `tabularx` at `\linewidth` with wrapping `L` columns is now used by **all 13 tables** in
Chapters 5, 6 and the appendix (`\usepackage{tabularx}` and the `L` column type are in
`parts/00_preamble_v3.tex`, to be folded into `settings.tex` at template merge). The note rows moved into
the captions, where they belong and where they wrap.

**Guard added:** `tools/check.py` now estimates table widths and warns about (a) any `\multicolumn` text
too wide to fit, since it cannot wrap, and (b) any plain `tabular` row wider than the text block. Tested
by restoring the old shape of Table 6.1: it reports *"about 20.0 cm wide"*.

## 3 — "Table 6.2 and more: there should be 3 envs, both-hard? Chapter 5 should clearly show the 3 variants"

**Chapter 5 now defines the three geometries.** New `tab:avoiding-geometries` gives, per geometry, the
halfspace lines, the keep-out disk, and how many of the 96 demonstrations satisfy it — with the shared
workspace, obstacles, goal line and the 0.025 m tightening in the caption. Read from
`config/projection_eval.yaml` with the geometry-to-constraint mapping of `scripts/eval.py:93-102`.

**Every obstacle-avoidance table now carries all three geometries.**

| table | before | now |
|---|---|---|
| `tab:state-headline` | one aggregated row per model | three geometries **and** the aggregate, per model |
| `tab:state-fm` | top-left-hard and top-right-hard only | all three, both-hard marked as one training seed on the baseline side |
| `tab:state-lowk` | top-left-hard only | all three |

🔴 **A claim that did not survive the third geometry.** The text said MeanFlow and consistency training
need *"fewer control steps and less time per episode"* than flow matching. Recomputed per geometry, the
cheapest configuration reaching S\&C $=1.00$ is:

| | top-left-hard | top-right-hard | both-hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| MeanFlow | 60.75 steps, 1.13 s, $\nfe=1$ | 60.60 steps, **12.64 s**, $\nfe=5$ | 58.90 steps, 1.58 s, $\nfe=2$ |
| flow matching | 64.40 steps, 1.30 s | 70.55 steps, **1.21 s** | 64.55 steps, **1.27 s** |

Fewer control steps holds on all three; **less time per episode holds only on top-left-hard**. The text
now says exactly that, and the summary row for obstacle avoidance in `tab:summary-models` reads *"fewer
steps on all three geometries; time per episode depends on the geometry"*.

Two further corrections that came with the recomputation: the old table's fixed configuration (budget 1,
temporal consistency) is **not** the cheapest full-success configuration for flow matching on
top-left-hard (random selection is, at 1.30 s), and its "cheapest" label was therefore wrong; and the
selection rule and budget now appear per row, because they differ per geometry.

## 4 — "Do the avoiding figures: env figures and the data trade-off figures"

Built in `Data_Analysis/DA_in_Paper` and exported into v3. **Reference code reused**, as asked:
`scripts/visualize_data_constraints.py` and `diffuser/utils/constraints_helpers.py` for the scene and the
constraint drawing, `scripts/eval.py` for the mapping, and the report `make_figs.py` loader for the
low-budget numbers.

| figure | group | what it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `fig_env_avoiding` | `env` | the task from above with the measured end-effector position of all **96 demonstrations**, the six obstacles and the goal line |
| `fig_constraints_avoiding` | `env` | the three geometries: excluded region shaded, tightened boundary dashed, keep-out disk with its tightened ring, demonstrations in grey, and *0, 1 and 2 of 96 satisfy it* per panel |
| `fig_avoiding_tradeoff` | `da` | control steps against time per control step, 2×2 (all three geometries, then each), every model at every budget and both selection rules, each point labelled with its budget |

**A new extraction step** (`plotting/extract/avoiding_scene.py`, `python3.14`) reads the configuration,
the geometry mapping and the D3IL dataset once and writes `data/avoiding_scene.json`; the builders stay
standard-library. It **asserts that `scripts/eval.py` still maps the geometries as assumed** and fails
loudly if not.

**A preview renderer** (`plotting/svg/preview_png.py`, PIL) was needed because the container has no SVG
rasteriser, so figures could otherwise only be seen after a LaTeX build. Every new figure was rendered
and inspected; it caught a legend overlapping its own protocol line, colliding budget labels, and text
too small for print (the constraint panels now use a 1.75 font scale, the trade-off grid 1.5).

The canvas gained filled polygons, data-unit circles, dashed lines, rectangular clipping, multi-panel
composition and a font scale. **The scale is a no-op at 1.0**: the five pre-existing figures are
byte-identical, checked with `cmp` before and after.

The four single-panel trade-off charts were replaced by the one grid and deleted from the store; the two
env figures left `PLANNED` in `sources.py`, which now lists only the alignment and quadrotor scenes.
