**Added — 🆕 §6.1.2.7 `sec:res:avoiding:budget20`, the last subsection before §6.1's conclusion**, as
the author asked. It answers the pending rows' question from data already on disk: each flow-based
model at $\nfe{=}2$ against $\nfe{=}20$ at the extended protocol, **matched to the evaluation cells
both budgets cover**, under the rule it is operated with, against the baseline of record.

| | S&C | steps | ms/step |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| FM $\nfe{=}2$ ($c$) → $\nfe{=}20$ | $1.000 \to 0.970$ | $68.8 \to 65.6$ | $27.3 \to 476.6$ (**17×**) |
| MeanFM $\nfe{=}2$ ($t$) → $\nfe{=}20$ | $0.990 \to 0.955$ | $61.7 \to 63.5$ | $27.3 \to 1003.9$ (**37×**) |
| CI-MeanFM $\nfe{=}2$ ($t$) → $\nfe{=}20$, seed 6 | $1.000 \to 0.950$ | $59.7 \to 62.4$ | $28.6 \to 1018.3$ (**36×**) |
| Diffusion $\nfe{=}20$ ($c$), the baseline | $0.983$ | $69.0$ | $563.5$ |

**No flow-based model improves at the baseline's budget**, and two of the three then cost *more* per
action than the baseline does there. That settles the standing objection that the comparison is run at
a budget that flatters the flow models: the advantage is a property of the objective **at a budget the
objective can be run at**, and a diffusion model cannot be moved to that budget without retraining.

- Matching matters and is stated in the caption: FM's $\nfe{=}20$ cell has *both-hard* for three seeds,
  so its $\nfe{=}2$ side is restricted to those three there — which is why $68.8$ appears here where
  `tab:state-headline` prints $68.4$ over every cell.
- A `\guard` records the two limits: CI-MeanFM is one training seed and carries no spread, and the
  thesis does **not** explain why MeanFM at twenty costs more per action than the baseline at twenty on
  cells that are not the same.
- R25, R26 and R18 in `PENDING_20260920_all_lacking_runs.md` are marked **ruled out on cost (author)**,
  with a new §10 recording the decision, the census above, and that running them would refine a number
  rather than open a question.
