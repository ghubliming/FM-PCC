## 3. "Genuine steps" renamed

*Genuine* is a value word: it says a step is real without saying what it does. The quantity counts
active steps that are **not** the final one — the only steps where a later step still exists for the
network to react to, so the only steps where projection steers the sample being generated instead of
correcting a finished one.

- §6.1.5 `Genuine Steps` → **`Steps at Which Projection Guides Sampling`**; the term is now the
  **guiding step count** $n\sidx{guide}$ (was $n_{\text{gen}}$, which also now uses the template's
  `\sidx` like the rest of the draft).
- Also in Chapter 8 (RQ3, twice) and the appendix name table, which keeps the artefact token
  `n_genuine` — that is what that table is for.
- The label `sec:method:degenerate` is **unchanged**, so §6.1.5's `\autoref` into Chapter 4 still
  resolves whether or not v2 acts.
- Recorded in `Auxiliary/Naming/TRANSLATION_20260914_dev_jargon_to_scientific.md` §3 (two rows).
- **Chapter 4 is v2's** and still defines "genuine step count", and still introduces the three cases as
  "regimes" — a banned word. Both are written up for v2 in
  [`handover/HANDOVER_20260915_for_v2_ch4_naming.md`](../handover/HANDOVER_20260915_for_v2_ch4_naming.md)
  and **not** edited here. Until v2 acts, Chapters 4 and 6 disagree in wording; that is the cost of not
  cross-editing.
