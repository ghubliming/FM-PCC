## 5 · §5.5.2 — the policy sizes, in detail, and the "under a million" claim checked

The old sentence — *"encoder–decoder transformers of two and four layers at a width of 64 (DDPM-ACT,
VAE-ACT) … each well under a million parameters"* — read as though one model had two layers and the other
four. **Both have a two-layer encoder and a four-layer decoder**; VAE-ACT carries a third two-layer stack,
the variational branch producing its latent (`act_vision_agent.yaml:29--58`,
`ddpm_encdec_vision_agent.yaml:40--59`).

**The suspicion is resolved: the claim holds, and by a wide margin.** D3IL publishes no parameter counts,
so these were computed from the shipped configuration and the block definitions in
`agents/models/act/act_vae.py` — $4(d^2{+}d) + (8d^2{+}5d) + 2d$ per encoder block, $+\,3(d^2{+}d)$ per
decoder block for the three cross-attention projections:

| head | shape | parameters |
| :-- | :-- | --: |
| BESO, DDPM-GPT | 4 layers, $d=72$ | $63.0$\,k/block → $\mathbf{0.25}$\,M |
| DDPM-ACT | enc 2 + dec 4, $d=64$ | $49.9$/$62.3$\,k per block → $\mathbf{0.35}$\,M |
| VAE-ACT | enc 2 + dec 4 + action-enc 2, $d=64$, plus embeddings and heads | $\mathbf{0.47}$\,M |

None of the four reaches half a million. The draft now says *"None of the four heads reaches half a million
parameters"* instead of the vaguer bound, prints the per-block arithmetic, and states the ratio that makes
the point: about $22$\,M of perception (two unshared ResNet-18 trunks) against under half a million of
policy — **some fifty times the head it feeds**.

## 6 · §5.5.2 — what D3IL calls *success*, and why this thesis never uses it

Stated exactly, from `environments/d3il/envs/gym_aligning_env/gym_aligning/envs/aligning.py:198--199,
334--346`. An episode succeeds there when **both** hold at once:

- box centre within $\mathbf{0.018}$\,m of the target centre, and
- orientation within $\mathbf{0.048\pi \approx 8.6^{\circ}}$ — the criterion is
  $2\arccos|q\sidx{box}\cdot q\sidx{target}| / \pi \le 0.048$ — which is what makes the coloured sides line
  up.

It is a $0/1$ count per episode, reported as a share. **This thesis defines no success rate on the
alignment task**: an episode is scored by the final distance from box to target in the table plane, as a
distance and never as a threshold, and orientation is not scored at all
(`sec:setup:metrics:aligning`, `tab:va-vs-d3il`). The D3IL figures are therefore read as a statement about
task difficulty and no cell of Chapter 6 is ever set against one. Two sentences that had drifted into
saying this twice were cut.
