## 3 · The preferred direction is now a legend element (author item 3)

`_dirarrow` in `plotting/builders/frontier.py` draws the arrow inside a **bordered box** with its label,
so a reader takes it for part of the key rather than for an annotation of a nearby point. It scales with
each figure's font factor and is placed in the corner the arrow points away from.

Two placements were corrected with it. On UAV-corridor the key moved to the top right, because the
bottom right is where the baseline point and its budget label sit. On D3IL-avoiding each panel's
y-limit gained **26 % headroom**, an empty band by construction, because at the old limits the box
landed on the diffusion points in two of the four panels. Every panel was inspected after raster export.

The three captions now name the key in one clause instead of repeating the direction in prose.

## 4 · Chapter 5 credits both works for the step metric (author item 4)

`sec:setup:metrics:avoiding` now says that control steps are a headline number in **both** works this
thesis measures against, and cites them: \ac{DPCC} reports the steps an episode takes beside its success
rates \parencite[Sec.~6.1]{romer2025diffusion}, and the endpoint-projection work reports, on this same
D3IL task, *Total Steps (Safe Trials)* — the average number of steps to reach the target on
constraint-satisfying episodes — as one of its three columns \parencite[Sec.~VII-A]{li2025hardflow}.
Verified in the paper itself, not from a summary.

## 5 · Fig. 6.4 rebuilt, and "wall clock" leaves the thesis (author item 5)

The figure carried a title, a subtitle in batch vocabulary (*"4 seeds (7-10) x 3 geometries x 2 trials =
24 rollouts per row"*), an axis reading *"wall clock per control step"* and 8 pt point labels. It is now
drawn at font factor 1.45 on a wider canvas with **no title and no subtitle**: the axes read
**step budget K** and **time to compute one action [s] (log)**, the point labels and legend are legible
at print size, and the protocol moved into the caption where the rest of the chapter keeps it.

**Vocabulary.** *Wall clock* describes how the measurement is taken, not what is reported. The thesis
name is defined once in Chapter 5 — **the time to compute one action**, written **time per control
step** on axes — and the five occurrences in Chapters 5 and 6 are replaced. Where the phrase meant an
episode's duration instead (the episode limit), it became **how long a single run may go on**. Two new
rows in `Auxiliary/Naming/TRANSLATION_20260914_dev_jargon_to_scientific.md` §6 record both, and the
second bans batch vocabulary (`rollout`, `trial`, "row", "cell") from inside a figure. v2's abstract and
v4's Chapter 8 each still carry one occurrence; both are raised in `cross_draft/INBOX.md` with
[`FROM_v3_20260920_v3.48_wall_clock_is_jargon.md`](../../cross_draft/to_v2/FROM_v3_20260920_v3.48_wall_clock_is_jargon.md).
