0. **The alignment constraint figure was wrong, and is now right.** It drew the workspace box, which is the
   reachable table area and not the task constraint. `combined_5` in
   `config/visual_aligning_eval.yaml:386--403` enforces **a halfspace** --- below the line through
   $(0.65,0.45)$ and $(0.80,-0.45)$, cutting off the far corner --- **and a circular keep-out region** of

   the box row is gone.
1. **New `fig:x2-dimensions`**, referenced from `tab:platforms`: the Skydio X2 from above, to scale from
   `quadrotor_modified.xml` --- four rotor disks of radius $0.13$\,m at $(\pm0.14,\pm0.18)$\,m, the body,

   projection inflates every obstacle by. Every number in the platform row is now visible as a shape, and

   spirit of the aggregated training table. The three per-environment subsections keep their labels but lose

   $0.0741$ (84\,\%) against the baseline's $0.4140$ (9\,\%). Both alignment tables, their captions, the
