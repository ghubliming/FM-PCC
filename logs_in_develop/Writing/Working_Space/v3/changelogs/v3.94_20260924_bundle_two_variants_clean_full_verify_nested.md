## `bundle/make_bundle.py` (the old tool is in the session scratchpad; git has it too)

**The two variants.** One `python3 bundle/make_bundle.py` now builds both.
- **Annotated, `thesis_v3_<stamp>_new`.** New sections only (v2 chapters collapsed); every drafting mark visible.
  Unchanged.
- **Clean, `thesis_v3_<stamp>_full_clean`.** The whole thesis. Its switch, `CLEAN_SWITCH`, is injected before
  `\begin{document}`:
  - `\submissiontrue` hides `\srcnote`;
  - `\dataref`, `\hole`, `\outdated` and `\flawed` are set to print nothing;
  - `\guard` and `\provisional` print their text as plain running text (a guard is the caveat sentence that travels
    with a claim, and a provisional statement is a claim, so both are thesis prose);
  - `\todofigure` keeps its space;
  - `\pillarsflawedfalse`;
  - a `deadblock` prints its content without banner or grey.

**Why the clean variant is full.** A collapsed v2 chapter prints a grey "v2 SECTION" box, which is itself a note. The
name therefore says `_full_clean`, and the tool's bundle pattern already matched it.

**Flags.**
- New: `--annotated-only` and `--clean-only`.
- Withdrawn: `--clean-notes` and `--no-notes`, with their switches.
- `--full` still gives the annotated complete document.

**The build prints what the clean variant switched off.** At this build: 76 `\srcnote`, 60 `\dataref`, 6 `\hole`,
1 `\outdated` and 2 `\flawed` print nothing; 21 `\guard` and 1 `\provisional` print as plain text.

**`--verify` now folds nested `\input` blocks back.** On the bundle side, the blank line `flatten()` adds, the child's
BEGIN..END banners and the blank line after become one token. On the source side, the `\input` line becomes that token,
with any pre/post text on its own lines, as `flatten()` writes it.
- Before this, `09_appendix.tex`, which inputs `app_ntrial20_feasible.tex` since v3.92, could never verify; v3.92 and
  v3.93 were hand-checked.
- Both new bundles now verify byte-faithful throughout: 10 inlined + 4 collapsed (`_new`), 14 inlined + 0 collapsed
  (`_full_clean`).

**`bundle/README.md`** now has the two-variant table and the new usage lines. The `--clean-notes` paragraph is replaced.
