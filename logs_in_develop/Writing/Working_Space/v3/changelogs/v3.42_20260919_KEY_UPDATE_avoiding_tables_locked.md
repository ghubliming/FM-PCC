## C · The full endpoint-vs-per-step study on D3IL-aligning (request 2)

New subsection §6.2.3.1 and new table `tab:va-projection-models`, covering the two models
`tab:va-projection` does not: **CI-MeanFM at $\nfe=2$ and $\nfe=20$, and FM at $\nfe=20$**, each with
both projectors under all three rules.

**The verdict, counted rather than asserted.** Eighteen comparisons run on the same model, budget and
rule — six new, twelve from `tab:va-projection`. Endpoint projection keeps more contexts free of
violations in **15**, per-step projection in **3**.

> The author asked to show that endpoint projection "totally beats" per-step here. It does not quite,
> and the draft says what the data says: **all three exceptions are under temporal consistency**, and two
> of them are one context. The claim that is supported, and is made: endpoint projection is the better
> projector on this task **wherever it has a guiding step**, by a margin that is consistent rather than
> large, and at a comparable price. The section also says plainly what it does *not* buy — the box is
> moved in as many contexts in four cells and fewer in two, and the median distances are level.

Cost, likewise not flattened: endpoint projection costs 0.6–0.9× per-step projection's time at $\nfe=10$
and $\nfe=20$, but about **three times as much** at $\nfe=2$, because one interior-point solve over the
whole trajectory does not get cheap merely because the trajectory took two steps.

Why this is the reverse of D3IL-avoiding is given as the mechanism, not as a surprise: this task is run
at ten and twenty evaluations because the generative model needs them, so the endpoint solve has guiding
steps; on D3IL-avoiding at one evaluation it had none.

**The "no MPC logic" clause of the request does not apply on this task.** Every aligning cell used is
`mpc4` and carries `dpcc-{r,c,t}`, so the per-step arm exists at full strength everywhere and needs no
excuse. The clause *does* apply on the s-curve — see §E.

A `\guard` records that neither CI-MeanFM nor FM moves the box at $\nfe=20$, which is what makes their
rows a clean read on the projector and also what empties their median-distance column.
