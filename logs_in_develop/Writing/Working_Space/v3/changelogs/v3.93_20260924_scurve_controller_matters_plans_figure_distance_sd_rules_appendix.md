## 1 · The controller conclusions (§6.3.3.3, §6.3.3.4, §6.4)

The v3.92 reading was "stability is the controller's limit, MuJoCo MPC lifts it". It is withdrawn: on the configuration
of record MPC is **not** the better controller. The author's conclusion now leads.

**The controller matters.** The same plans succeed or fail by the controller that flies them:
- projected: 5/10 against 0/10 successes;
- unprojected: 23.0 against 41.5 violating steps.

**Our cascaded geometric controller is the better one:**
- equal success unprojected (9/10 each);
- 5/10 against 0/10 projected;
- fewer violating steps in both;
- 1/24 of the cost per control step.

MPC's one gain, keeping the unprojected flights upright and within 0.3 m of the goal, does not survive projection, and MPC
is not adopted.
