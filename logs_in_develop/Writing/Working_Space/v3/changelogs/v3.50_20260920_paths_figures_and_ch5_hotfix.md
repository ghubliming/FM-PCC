## 4 · 🆕 The two missing executed-path figures are built

This had been asked for four times and was blocked on a builder that was never written. It is written.

**Pipeline**, the same split the quadrotor path figures use: `plotting/extract/exec_paths.py` (numpy,
reads the gitignored `temp/20-09` and `temp/19-09` drops) → `data/exec_paths.json` →

**The constraint set under a path is not redrawn.** Two panels were factored out —
`avoiding.geometry_panel` and `scenes.aligning_constraint_panel`, the manipulator equivalents of
`scenes._uav_constraint_panel` — so what lies under a path is the geometry the projector was given,
drawn by the same code as the Chapter 5 environment figures.

### `fig:avoiding-paths` — §6.1.2.2

Four panels, 20 episodes each: MeanFM, CI-MeanFM and FM at one network evaluation and the diffusion
baseline at twenty, all under per-step projection with temporal consistency, tightened, on
`halfspace_top-right-hard`, training seed 6.

**All 80 episodes are violation-free**; 19, 17, 17 and 20 of 20 reach the goal. That uniformity is the
result: the plans entering this figure are the near-scribbles of the $\nfe=1$ row of Fig. 6.1, and
between the two figures sits the projector.

- `top-right-hard` because it is the geometry that separates the models (§6.1.1) **and** the one all
  four cover — the diffusion `both-hard` artefact is the cell the truncated job never wrote.
- `\guard`: one seed, one geometry — a single cell of the extended protocol, so the panel counts are
  not the five-seed rates of `tab:state-headline`.

### `fig:aligning-paths` — §6.2

Three panels, the ten contexts, under no projection / per-step / endpoint; analytic average-velocity
matching at $\nfe=20$, random selection, tightened, seed 6.

**Violation-free counts 2/10, 9/10 and 10/10 — which reproduce the $\nfe=20$ rows of
`tab:va-projection` exactly.** An independent check that the rollout artefacts and the published table
describe the same evaluation.

Measured while building and now in the prose, because it is sharper than "nine of ten": on the
unprojected arm **seven** contexts enter the keep-out region and an eighth leaves the halfspace; under
**either** projector neither the region nor the halfspace is entered in any context, and the single
context per-step projection does not keep violation-free breaches the **action box** instead.

- 🔴 `\guard`: **this draws the end effector, not the box.** The constraint set applies to the planned
  end-effector position, so the drawn quantity is the one the projector acts on — but the quadrotor
  figures draw the constrained *body*, and the equivalent here is the box, which the evaluation does
  not log. That is **D10c**, an evaluation re-run, and the guard says so.
