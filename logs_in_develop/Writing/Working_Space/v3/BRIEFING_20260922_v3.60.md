## 2 · The one thing worth understanding properly

**Why the diffusion baseline scores 0/12 on UAV-corridor while flying the corridor.**

The finish line is **not** a vertical plane at the end of the corridor. It is the plane through the
demonstrated route's endpoint, **perpendicular to that route's final approach heading** — and the
demonstrated routes *turn downward* as they exit.

Projected, the baseline holds the lane it learned and never makes that turn. So it:

- travels as far along *x* as the models that pass (ends at x = 2.66–2.79 m; passing flights end at 2.55–2.98),
- stays on the near side of the line,
- runs the **full 396-step episode limit** on every flight instead of stopping on arrival,
- ends **0.51 m** from the goal, collision-free.

"Collision-free and 0/12" is not a contradiction: the projector kept it legal by keeping it on a lane
that does not reach the goal.

**The figure was genuinely misleading.** The dashes *were* there — but at `6,4` on a 1.9-wide stroke,
twelve near-coincident flights fill in each other's gaps and the bundle renders solid. Now dotted on a
thinner stroke with a much larger hollow end mark. Re-rendered and checked.

## 6 · The writing rules this draft is held to

Short version, for whoever picks this up next — you, me, or another agent.

**Tone.** Write as the person who ran the experiments. State the result; do not defend it.
A limit stated plainly is strength; a limit argued around is not.

🔴 **Red alert — the prompt is not thesis text.** No prompt rephrasing, no justification tails, no
"as requested". If a sentence only exists because someone asked for the thing, delete it.

🟡 **Yellow alert — no self-reasoning.** No *because* the evaluation did not produce. No arguing with an
objection nobody raised. (The resolution paragraph deleted in §6.1.2.1 was a textbook case.)

**Other standing rules**

- **No dev jargon.** "Flagship" is banned. "Wall-clock time" → *the time to compute one action*.
- **Brands only in Related Work.** Elsewhere, mechanism names.
- **No p-values, no significance tests.** Counts, means, and the spread you actually measured.
- **Never "better" without axes.** Pareto-dominant, or say "trade-off".
- **Every number names its evidence** — a batch directory, or a repo file with line numbers.
- **A budget goes in a table only if it carries the full protocol.** If it is short, say so or leave it out.
- **Twenty-episode material is supplementary**, appendix only, never main-text evidence.
- **"Checked" never means "compiled".** There is no TeX toolchain in this container.
