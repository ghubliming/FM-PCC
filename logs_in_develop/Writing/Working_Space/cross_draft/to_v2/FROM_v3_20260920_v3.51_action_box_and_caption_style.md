## 1. The action box is out of Chapter 5 — please check Chapter 4

**The author's instruction (2026-09-20), verbatim in substance:** the action box is not thesis
material. It is an `action_bounds: 'auto'` bound derived at evaluation time from the per-channel range
of the demonstrated increments, so it carries **no value a reader can check**, and no table of the
benchmark it is inherited from states one. Naming it in a constraint table invites the question
*"which range?"* and the thesis has no answer to print.

**What v3 did at v3.51.** Removed it from:

- `tab:aligning-constraints` (the *Action box — range of the commanded increment — the per-channel
  range of the demonstrated increments* row), and the caption sentence that excused it for carrying
  no number;
- the `combined-5` sentence of §5.2.2 and the intro sentence *"a constraint set on the planned
  end-effector position **and on the action**"*;
- the `Constraints` row of `tab:va-vs-d3il` (now: *the constraint set of Table 5.3, enforced by the
  projection*);
- the `\srcnote`s that quoted `action_bounds: 'auto'`;
- the UAV-corridor protocol sentence (*"releases the action box"* → *"leaves the action
  unconstrained"*);
- the D3IL-avoiding constraint sentence, which pointed at `sec:method:env:avoiding` for it.

**What is left in v2.** `04_method.tex`, in the alignment environment:

> *"Its constraint set is a workspace box on the planned end-effector position ... **and a box on the
> action given by the range of the demonstrated actions**"*

and the same construction in the obstacle-avoidance environment. The `\srcnote` there quotes
`action_bounds: 'auto'`.

**Suggested action.** Chapter 4 is the method chapter, so a bound the solver really does carry may
legitimately be *defined* there — that is v2's call. But if it stays, it should be the only place it
appears, and it should say what it is (a bound fitted to the demonstrations at evaluation time)
rather than reading as a constraint with values the thesis never gives. Chapter 5 no longer forwards
a reader to it.

> The halfspace contradiction in the same paragraph (*"the table carries no walls, so the task has no
> halfspaces"* vs. the evaluated `combined_5`, which has one) was already filed by v3.36 —
> [`FROM_v3_20260918_aligning_observation_and_constraints.md`](FROM_v3_20260918_aligning_observation_and_constraints.md).
> It is still open and sits two lines from the action-box sentence, so both can be fixed in one pass.

the excluded region stays readable underneath. That leaves 0, 1 and 2 green lines of the 96, which is
why satisfying the constraints can only be the projection's doing"*) and ruled it out of the thesis:

> *"this is my prompt, why it is inside the thesis? this is self talking and prompt rephasing! ...
> instead just fact telling. no mention, describe the fig, do the proper thesis writing."*

**[`../../Writing_Hints/HINT_20260920_prompt_is_not_thesis_text.md`](../../Writing_Hints/HINT_20260920_prompt_is_not_thesis_text.md)**,
