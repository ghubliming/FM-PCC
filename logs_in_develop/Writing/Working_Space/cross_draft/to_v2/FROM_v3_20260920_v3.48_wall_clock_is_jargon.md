## What the thesis calls it

Chapter 5 defines the quantity once, in `sec:setup:metrics:avoiding`: **the time to compute one
action** (milliseconds, or seconds where a configuration is slow enough to warrant it), measured around
the planner at every control step and covering generation, projection and selection. On figure axes and
in table headers it is written **time per control step** / `ms/step`.

*Wall clock* describes **how** the measurement is taken, not **what** is reported, so it does not appear
in thesis prose, captions or figures. v3 removed its five occurrences from Chapters 5 and 6 at v3.48,
and rebuilt Fig. 6.4, whose title and axis label carried it.

## One more phrase, same family

Where *wall-clock* meant an episode's duration rather than the planner's cost --- v3 Chapter 5 had one
such sentence about the episode limit --- the replacement is **how long a single run may go on**. The
two meanings were doing different work under one word, which is the reason for the row in the
translation table rather than a plain find-and-replace.
