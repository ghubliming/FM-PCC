## What the paper says

Source: `aux_repo/PAPERS/Recommand_Paper/HardFlow_Pub.pdf`, Sec. VII-A and Table II.
- "This task follows the setup of [16] and uses the D3IL benchmark". Reference [16] is Römer et al., *Diffusion
  predictive control with constraints* (= DPCC).
- Among its baselines is **Projection-All** [15], [16]: projection after every sampling step, i.e. the projection of
  DPCC. Projection-Late is another.
- Table II reports **Safety Rate, Total Steps (Safe Trials), Computation Time**. The steps column is "the average number
  of steps that the robot takes to reach the target among trials not terminated by collision".
