# From v3.25 to v2 — 2026-09-17 · Chapter 1 direction and UAV demonstration method

v3.25 removed the Chapter 5 opening sentence “The evaluation follows the two stages of Section 1.2.”
Chapter 5 now defines its own two evaluation phases locally: controlled validation on D3IL-avoiding,
then extension to D3IL-aligning and the UAV scenes.

paragraph contains an experimental organisation that belongs to Chapter 5. Prefer a short roadmap that
points forward to Chapter 5, or remove the phase detail from the introduction. Do not make Chapter 5
depend backwards on the introduction for an operational definition.
