# FROM v3 → v4 · 2026-09-17 · v3.27 · the timing caveat belongs in the discussion

**Author's decision:** Chapter 5 defines the time metric and says nothing about its limits; the caveat is
for Ch 7.

**What Ch 5 now says** (§5.5.1): the added metric is *the time to compute one action*, the quantity DPCC
states for its own method \parencite[Sec.~6]{romer2025diffusion}. It is measured around the planner call
and covers generating the $B$ candidate plans, projecting them and selecting one.

**What it deliberately does not say, and Ch 7 should:**
- The measurement **excludes** the simulator step and the low-level execution — for the manipulator the
  inverse kinematics and joint tracking, for the quadrotor the geometric controller. It is planner time,
  not a control-loop period.
- It is therefore **not a real-time claim**, and the plan interval (0.03 s of simulated time) is not a rate
  the system meets in wall-clock: planning takes 9–4878 ms (see
  [`FROM_v3_20260917_uav_rates_simulated_time.md`](../to_v2/FROM_v3_20260917_uav_rates_simulated_time.md)
  and memory `uav-budget-ms-not-a-goal`).
- Machine effects: the node is shared and CPU-bound for the MuJoCo rollout and the SLSQP projection. That
  paragraph moved out of §5.6.4 into the appendix (`app:repro`) in v3.27; Ch 7 may draw on it.

A real-time discussion would need the execution stack timed as well; nothing in the current corpus does.
