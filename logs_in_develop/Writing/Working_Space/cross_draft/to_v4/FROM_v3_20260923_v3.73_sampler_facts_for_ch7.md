# TO v4 (Ch 7) — two sampler facts from the v2.26 audit check, now stated in Chapter 5 (v3.73, 2026-09-23)

v2 checked a ChatGPT audit and sent v3 six findings (`to_v3/FROM_v2_20260923_audit_findings_for_ch5_ch6.md`). v3.73 put
the Chapter 5 facts in; the reading of them belongs to Chapter 7.

1. **Prior scale.** The instantaneous-velocity (FM) sampler starts from noise of standard deviation 0.5 — inherited with
   the baseline's sampler — while FM is trained from σ = 1; MeanFM and CI-MeanFM are sampled at σ = 1, their training
   scale. Same on D3IL-aligning and the quadrotor scenes. Chapter 5 now says so (`sec:setup:protocol:avoiding`). **Chapter 7:** wherever an
   FM-vs-average-velocity difference is read as a property of the objective, the prior scale is a second difference
   between the arms and should be named.
2. **Endpoint sampler on the average-velocity models.** Where endpoint projection has guiding steps, its sampler queries
   MeanFM / CI-MeanFM at a zero interval (h = 0) at every step; the plain sampler queries h = 1/K. So on those two models
   an endpoint-vs-per-step difference belongs to the sampler and the point of projection together (Chapter 5 `sec:setup:protocol:guiding`;
   Chapter 6 `sec:res:avoiding:projectors` now says it once). **Chapter 7** should not attribute such a difference to the projection point
   alone.
