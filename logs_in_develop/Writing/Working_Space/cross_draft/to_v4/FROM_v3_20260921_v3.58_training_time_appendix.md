and **not be deleted** when v4 takes ownership. Preserve `app:training-time-laws` in

$p(\tau)=1.5(1-\tau)^{1/2}$; an earlier v2 handover note incorrectly said the opposite and has been
superseded by `to_v2/FROM_v3_20260921_v3.58_shapes_and_reflected_time.md`.
