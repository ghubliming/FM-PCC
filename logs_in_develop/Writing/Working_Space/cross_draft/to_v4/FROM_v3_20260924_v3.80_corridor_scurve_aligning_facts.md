## 2 · The corridor in one paragraph (for Ch 7/8)

Unprojected, every model crosses on every flight and none is violation-free (tilt 74.5–99.4, hump 28.6–36.6 violating
steps per flight). Projected, the vehicle makes the altitude change the constraint asks for (descends under the tilt on
540 of 560 flights, median 0.12–0.37 m; climbs over the hump on all 560, median 0.27–0.39 m). Two structural results:
(i) **tilt hand-over residue** — every violating step lies in the plane's last 0.3 m (x 1.71–2.00); at the first one the
commanded position is already past the plane's end (x 2.27–2.52, 0.49–0.61 m ahead) and clear of the constraint at every
violating step: the span of the plane is checked at the commanded position, so the plan is released before the vehicle
has left the plane; the budget makes the residue shallower (8.8–11.9 cm at K ≤ 2 → 1.0–2.2 cm at K = 20), never zero.
(ii) **hump stop** — under per-step projection at K ≤ 2 none of 180 flow flights crosses: 174 stop 0.01–0.17 m before
the apex on the step cap, 6 drift sideways across the wall's boundary; plans never rise above 1.30 m (the demonstrations'
range) while the apex needs 1.48 m, and the rising roof face binds the whole plan while the commanded position is short
of the apex. From K = 3 every flight crosses. The hole's expected shape ("average-velocity ahead of FM on the
constraint") is **not** in the data.
