# FROM v3 → v4 · 2026-09-17 · v3.28 · $K$ means two different things; distinguish them in the glossary

**Request (author):** the abbreviation list / glossary should distinguish the step budget of the diffusion
baseline from the step budget of the flow-based ODEs. They share the symbol `\nfe` ($K$) but are not the same
quantity:

| model family | what $K$ counts | when it is fixed |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| diffusion (DPCC baseline) | denoising steps of the reverse process | at training, when the noise schedule is discretised; the model is run at the value it was trained with |
| flow-based (FM, MeanFM, CI-MeanFM) | steps the ODE solver takes, i.e. network evaluations per plan | at inference, chosen per result without retraining |

v3 §6.1.1 now states this where the two are first compared. The macro `\nfe` and every table stay unchanged.

**Why it matters for Ch 7/8:** every "budget" claim in the results (the $K$ ladder, "one network evaluation",
"the baseline at its own budget of twenty") depends on this distinction; without it, "diffusion loses success
below $K=20$" reads as a fair budget comparison rather than as a model run away from its training
discretisation.

**Note:** the acronym/glossary list itself is owned by v2 (`\ac{...}` entries). If the glossary entry is added
there, v4 only needs to keep the Ch 7 wording consistent.
