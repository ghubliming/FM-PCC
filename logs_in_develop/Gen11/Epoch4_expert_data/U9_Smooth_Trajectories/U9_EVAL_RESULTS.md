## 4. Root cause

The `blended_path` U9 redesign eliminated stop-and-go correctly for LLL/RRR and for all non-pillar scenes. The failure is isolated to LRL/RLR at the shortest durations (T near 10s):

- **Centripetal acceleration** at the diagonal-exit fillet = `ṡ²/r` = (π·L/2T)²/r ≈ 8.6 m/s² at T=10s, r=0.30m.
- The cascaded PID's thrust-priority allocator saturates at ~3–4 g effective lateral; 8.6 m/s² (≈0.88 g lateral alone) + gravity + tracking dynamics exceeded the allocation budget.
- The fillet geometry was confirmed correct by `verify_blends.py` (clearance gate ≥ 0.43m met) — the failure is PID tracking, not path geometry.
