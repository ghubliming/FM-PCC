Read columns by name: the three raw-table column orders differ. Pivot `metric`/`value`
only after selecting candidate, seed, geometry, variant and protocol; native aggregate tables
also contain masks that must remain separate. Candidate IDs are local to a batch: match an old
report by full path and settings, not by reusing its candidate number. Preserve backbone,
parameter count, K, activation threshold, fan, training configuration and geometry in comparisons.
Do not use the global candidate ranking as a paper ranking across different tasks or protocols.
No training, evaluation, or figure regeneration was performed for this ledger.
